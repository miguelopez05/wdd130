Manaus, Brazil

Manaus is the capital of the state of Amazonas. It is the seventh most populous city in Brazil and the sixth largest urban area in the country. Its metropolitan region, with more than 2.8 million inhabitants, is the 11th most populous in the country. A major tourist destination in the Amazon, its greatest cultural symbol is the Amazonas Theatre, listed as a National Historical Heritage site by IPHAN.
